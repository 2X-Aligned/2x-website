Privacy Policy

Last updated July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 2X Aligned LLC (“2X Aligned”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit 2xaligned.com or the websites of our portfolio brands, when you receive marketing communications from us or our brands, or when you otherwise interact with us (collectively, the “Services”).

2X Aligned is a marketing technology company that operates marketing and email-outreach programs for its own portfolio of brands. Because of that, this policy covers two groups of people: visitors to our websites and business contacts who receive our marketing outreach.

If you have any questions, or want to exercise any of the rights described below, contact us at privacy@2xaligned.com.

1. What Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

If you contact us by email, fill out a form, or otherwise communicate with us, we collect the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

Information we collect automatically

When you visit our websites, we and our hosting providers collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and timestamps. When we send email, we collect delivery and engagement data such as whether a message was delivered, bounced, opened, or clicked, and whether the recipient unsubscribed or complained. We use this to measure performance, maintain list hygiene, and honor opt-outs.

Business contact information we obtain from other sources

To conduct business-to-business marketing outreach for our brands, we license professional contact records from reputable commercial B2B data providers. These records are limited to business-related information, such as:

  • Name and job title;
  • Business email address and business phone number;
  • Employer name, industry, and company details;
  • Business location and public professional profile information.

Before any outreach, every contact record passes through our automated hygiene pipeline, which includes syntax validation, role-account filtering, disposable-domain detection, and deliverability verification. Anyone who bounces, complains, or opts out is added to a permanent suppression list that applies across all of our brands.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send business-to-business marketing communications about our portfolio brands;
  • Respond to your inquiries and communicate with you;
  • Process and permanently honor unsubscribe and suppression requests;
  • Monitor deliverability, measure campaign performance, and maintain the quality and accuracy of our contact data;
  • Operate, secure, and improve our websites and sending infrastructure, including fraud and abuse prevention; and
  • Comply with legal obligations, including CAN-SPAM, GDPR, and state privacy laws.

3. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we process personal information on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — for business-to-business marketing to professional contacts about products and services relevant to their role, and for securing and improving the Services. We balance these interests against your rights, limit data to business contact information, and provide a simple opt-out in every message.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) — where required, for example where local law requires consent for electronic marketing. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) — where processing is necessary to comply with the law, such as maintaining suppression lists.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information, and we have not sold or shared personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising in the preceding twelve months. We share personal information only with:

  • Service providers (processors) that help us operate the Services under contract, including Sinch Mailgun (email delivery), Google Cloud Platform (hosting and data infrastructure), and GitHub (website hosting). These providers may only process data on our instructions.
  • Our portfolio brands, which operate under this policy and share our central suppression list, so an opt-out from one brand is honored by all of them.
  • Legal and safety recipients, if required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of 2X Aligned, our users, or others.
  • Business transfer parties, in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to previously collected data.

5. How to Opt Out of Marketing Email

Every marketing email we send includes a functioning one-click unsubscribe link and our physical postal address. When you unsubscribe:

  • Your request is processed automatically and promptly;
  • Your address is added to a permanent suppression list; and
  • The suppression applies across all 2X Aligned brands, not just the one that emailed you.

You can also opt out, or ask what data we hold about you, by emailing privacy@2xaligned.com at any time.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Engagement and delivery logs are retained for deliverability and compliance auditing. Suppression records (the minimal data needed to keep honoring an opt-out) are retained indefinitely, because deleting them would risk contacting you again. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

7. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and monitoring of our sending infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Transfers

We are based in the United States and process data there. If you are located outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses with our service providers.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know whether we process your personal information and access a copy of it;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Request deletion of your personal information;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, including direct marketing (you can object to marketing at any time and we will stop);
  • Receive your data in a portable format; and
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@2xaligned.com. We will verify your request using the email address concerned or other reasonable means, and respond within the time required by applicable law (30 days in most cases). You may use an authorized agent, provided the agent can demonstrate valid authorization to act on your behalf.

California residents

If you are a California resident, the CCPA/CPRA gives you the rights listed above with respect to the categories of personal information we collect (identifiers and professional information, as described in Section 1). We collect this information from the sources and for the purposes described in Sections 1 and 2, and disclose it only to the service providers described in Section 4. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. California’s “Shine the Light” law (Civil Code § 1798.83) requests can be sent to privacy@2xaligned.com.

EEA, UK, and Swiss residents

If you believe our processing of your personal information violates applicable law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. We would appreciate the chance to address your concerns first — please contact us at privacy@2xaligned.com.

10. Do-Not-Track Signals

Our websites do not currently respond to browser Do-Not-Track signals, as no uniform standard has been adopted. If a standard is established, we will update this policy accordingly.

11. Children

Our Services are directed at businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have collected information from a minor, contact us at privacy@2xaligned.com and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision, and the updated version is effective as soon as it is posted. Material changes will be prominently noted on this page.
